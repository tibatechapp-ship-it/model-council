# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, constructive, and inclusive
environment for everyone contributing to MODEL-COUNCIL.

## Expected behavior

- Treat participants with respect.
- Welcome constructive criticism and different perspectives.
- Support evidence-based technical and scientific discussion.
- Give appropriate credit to contributors and prior research.
- Report security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation,
and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Reports may be submitted privately through the repository's
available security or maintainer contact channels.

This policy applies to project spaces and activities representing the project.
