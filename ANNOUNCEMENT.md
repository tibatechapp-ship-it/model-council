# MODEL-COUNCIL
## A Vision for Collaborative Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is advancing rapidly.

MODEL-COUNCIL explores an ambitious idea: different AI models working together, exchanging perspectives, examining ideas, challenging conclusions, and contributing to more transparent and evidence-informed outcomes.

We are building an open-source foundation for collaborative AI.

The project is at an early stage, and we are sharing this vision to connect with people and organizations interested in the future of artificial intelligence.

We welcome conversations with:

- Technology companies
- Universities and research institutions
- AI researchers and developers
- Investors and potential strategic partners
- Organizations interested in adoption and collaboration

We are not making a fixed funding request or proposing predefined terms.

If this vision aligns with your interests, we welcome a conversation to explore potential collaboration, partnership, or adoption opportunities.
