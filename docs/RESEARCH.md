# Research and Institutional Collaboration

MODEL-COUNCIL welcomes collaboration with:

- Universities and academic research groups.
- AI and machine learning researchers.
- Independent research laboratories.
- Software and infrastructure engineers.
- Security and evaluation specialists.
- Students and open-source contributors.

## Research directions

Potential areas of investigation include:

1. Independent multi-model reasoning and consultation.
2. Cross-model disagreement and uncertainty measurement.
3. Evidence verification and source attribution.
4. Bias, reliability, and model evaluation.
5. Secure orchestration of external AI providers.
6. Reproducible evaluation methods and benchmarks.

## Collaboration principles

Research contributions should document their methodology, assumptions,
limitations, and evaluation criteria.

Claims must be supported by reproducible evidence whenever possible.
Model agreement must not be treated as proof of correctness.

## Participation

Open a Research Proposal issue or submit a pull request describing
the proposed work.

Institutional partnerships and funding arrangements are not implied
by this invitation; any formal agreements will be announced explicitly.
