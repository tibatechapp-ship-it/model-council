# Support MODEL-COUNCIL Research & Development

MODEL-COUNCIL is an open-source initiative to improve multi-model AI consultation, structured debate, and evidence-based verification.

## How to Support

### Research
- Propose evaluation methodologies.
- Study model agreement and disagreement.
- Investigate hallucination and uncertainty.
- Publish reproducible benchmarks.

### Engineering
- Develop provider integrations.
- Improve orchestration and reliability.
- Build verification and evaluation tools.
- Strengthen privacy and security.

### Community
- Test the project.
- Improve documentation.
- Report bugs.
- Review contributions.

## Institutional Collaboration

We welcome collaboration with researchers, universities, laboratories, independent developers, and open-source communities.

## Funding and Sponsorship

Financial sponsorship and infrastructure support may be discussed as the project develops. No donation or funding channel is claimed to be active unless officially published in this repository.

Every contribution helps advance transparent and reliable AI research.
